LUMINISKIN WARRANTY

Return and warranty policy
- Customers may report issues related to damaged packaging, incorrect items, or products received in poor condition
- Reports should be made within 48 hours of delivery
- The customer may be asked to provide photos of the package and product

Accepted review cases
- Wrong item received
- Product damaged during delivery
- Missing item in order

Not automatically covered
- Opened product already used extensively
- Personal dislike of texture or scent
- Skin reactions that require professional medical evaluation

Resolution options
- Replacement product
- Store credit
- Review by support team for special cases

Important rules for the AI seller
- Do not approve refunds automatically
- Do not promise compensation without human review
- For complaints, refund requests, or strong dissatisfaction, escalate to human support
- If a customer reports a severe skin reaction, advise them to stop using the product and contact a healthcare professional, then escalate to support
